### 1. 3D & Mixed Reality (3D 與混合實境)
*   **風格核心：** 數位物件進入實體空間的「物件感」，強調虛擬與現實的物理邊界模糊。
*   **視覺風格與材質：** 玻璃擬態 (Glassmorphism)、透明壓克力、等距視角 (Isometric)、懸浮 3D 建模、數位介面疊加感。
*   **色彩配置：** 深邃背景 (Dark Mode) 配上高飽和的科技色（如電力藍、霓虹紫、螢光綠）。
*   **AI 生成推薦關鍵字 (Prompts)：**
    > `Product design, a transparent plastic blister packaging box floating in a dark void. Inside the box is a miniature isometric 3D glowing gaming room, featuring neon purple and cyan lighting. Mixed reality concept, translucent digital UI elements hovering around. Hyper-realistic, Octane Render, ray tracing, studio lighting, highly detailed, 8k. --ar 16:9`
*   **打光與引擎：** `Ray tracing, Octane Render, Unreal Engine 5, Volumetric lighting.`

### 2. Experimental Typography (實驗性字體藝術)
*   **風格核心：** 文字不僅是資訊，而是具備物理特性的 3D 雕塑，強調「膨脹、扭曲、擠壓」的力學美。
*   **視覺風格與材質：** 充氣氣球材質、鏡面金屬、液態流體字、極度拉伸的字形 (Stretched font)。
*   **色彩配置：** 糖果色系（粉紅、亮藍）與中性背景的強烈對比，或加入少許螢光色作為點綴。
*   **AI 生成推薦關鍵字 (Prompts)：**
    > `Experimental 3D typography, the word "LOVE" rendered as highly inflated, puffy balloon-like letters. Glossy plastic texture squeezing against each other. Vibrant bubblegum pink and electric blue colors. Pitch black background. High contrast, dynamic composition, 3D render, Cinema 4D, playful and distorted typographic art. --ar 4:3`
*   **打光與引擎：** `High contrast lighting, Dynamic shadows, Glossy reflections.`

### 3. Neo-Minimalism (新極簡主義)
*   **風格核心：** 「少即是多」的極限化表現。利用極致的留白 (Negative Space) 來聚焦微小的主體，展現高度自信與冷靜。
*   **視覺風格與材質：** 高質感紙張紋理、銳利的幾何邊緣、優雅的現代襯線體 (Serif)、雜誌排版感。
*   **色彩配置：** 單色調為主（黑白灰），偶爾使用單一的醒目純色（如愛馬仕橘或克萊因藍）。
*   **AI 生成推薦關鍵字 (Prompts)：**
    > `Neo-minimalism layout. Extreme generous white space. A tiny, stark black silhouette of a lonely person at the bottom center. Oversized, elegant modern serif typography acting as the main title. Microscopic UI navigation icons at the corners. High-end editorial aesthetic, strictly black and white, ultra-clean. --ar 16:9 --style raw`
*   **打光與引擎：** `Soft even lighting, Flat aesthetic, High-key lighting.`

### 4. Surreal Visual Storytelling (超現實視覺說故事)
*   **風格核心：** 邏輯崩壞的「夢境寫實」。將寫實的人像或物件放置於不可能的場景中，營造奇異的視覺敘事。
*   **視覺風格與材質：** 拼貼藝術、數位合成、自然元素（花、昆蟲）與人類的結合、復古膠卷質感。
*   **色彩配置：** 高飽和度的超現實配色，常見對比色組合（如青色皮膚配橘色蝴蝶）。
*   **AI 生成推薦關鍵字 (Prompts)：**
    > `Surreal visual storytelling, a photorealistic portrait of a woman with teal blue skin. A giant, highly detailed orange butterfly covers her eyes as a mask. Surrounded by vintage wildflowers. Warm cream background. Dreamscape, bizarre but beautiful, high-fashion editorial photography, sharp focus, cinematic lighting. --ar 16:9`
*   **打光與引擎：** `Cinematic lighting, Moody atmosphere, Dreamy haze.`

### 5. Motion as Identity (動態作為品牌識別)
*   **風格核心：** 賦予靜態視覺「流動感」。捕捉變形中的瞬間，強調生命力與適應性。
*   **視覺風格與材質：** 動態模糊 (Motion Blur)、液態金屬、圓潤的漸層管狀流體、像絲帶般的連續路徑。
*   **色彩配置：** 無縫銜接的彩虹漸層、極光色、變色龍漆面感。
*   **AI 生成推薦關鍵字 (Prompts)：**
    > `Kinetic identity design, an abstract logo shaped like a fluid ribbon morphing into a heart. Vibrant seamless gradient of magenta, neon yellow, and cyan. Translucent glossy plastic texture, soft 3D shading. Pitch black background. Sense of continuous fluid motion, sleek modern render. --ar 1:1`
*   **打光與引擎：** `Glossy finish, Iridescent lighting, 60fps motion blur effect.`

### 6. Eco Aesthetic & Earthy Neutral (環保美學與大地中性色)
*   **風格核心：** 視覺上的「觸覺化」。強調不完美、手工感與大自然的原始質地。
*   **視覺風格與材質：** 回收紙紋理、粗獷的顏料刷痕、石頭與黏土感、植物斑駁陰影。
*   **色彩配置：** 低飽和大地色調：鼠尾草綠 (Sage)、赤陶色 (Terracotta)、燕麥色、灰土色。
*   **AI 生成推薦關鍵字 (Prompts)：**
    > `Eco-aesthetic, four minimalist paper perfume boxes. Muted earthy neutral palette: oat beige, sage green, and terracotta. Raw unbleached paper texture. Tactile thick organic acrylic paint brush strokes on the boxes. Minimalist thin black typography. Soft natural dappled sunlight, leaf shadows, photorealistic textures. --ar 16:9`
*   **打光與引擎：** `Natural dappled sunlight, Soft shadows, Realistic textures.`

### 7. Modular & Grid-Based Layouts (模組化與網格佈局)
*   **風格核心：** 將設計的「骨架」外露。利用嚴謹的網格切割畫面，平衡資訊量與美感。
*   **視覺風格與材質：** 便當盒佈局 (Bento Box)、極細分割線、結構化區塊、建築圖紙感。
*   **色彩配置：** 溫潤的中性底色配上高對比的照片內容，強調秩序感。
*   **AI 生成推薦關鍵字 (Prompts)：**
    > `Modern modular bento box grid system. Warm sand beige background. Divided by crisp, thin 1px black lines into neat rectangular compartments. Inside the grids are high-fashion desert photographs and minimalist text blocks. Structured editorial aesthetic, strict geometric organization, clean and precise. --ar 16:9`
*   **打光與引擎：** `Flat design, Crisp vector lines, Even studio light.`

### 8. Cyber Pastel & Futuristic Gradients (賽博粉彩與未來漸層)
*   **風格核心：** 柔和的高科技質感。捨棄攻擊性的霓虹，改用明亮、通透的 3D 渲染來展現「友善的未來感」。
*   **視覺風格與材質：** 毛玻璃 (Frosted Glass)、全息投影 (Holographic)、液態發光核心、極簡 3D 幾何。
*   **色彩配置：** 粉彩色系結合數位光感（粉藍、蜜桃橘、淡紫色），強調色彩的通透與流動感。
*   **AI 生成推薦關鍵字 (Prompts)：**
    > `Futuristic 3D graphic, an abstract twisted torus ring floating. Made of frosted translucent dark blue glass with a luminous fluid gradient of electric pink and peach inside. Midnight blue background. Soft neon cyber pastel illumination, holographic reflections. Sleek futuristic UI lines. Octane Render, 8k. --ar 16:9`
*   **打光與引擎：** `Subsurface scattering (SSS), Ethereal glow, Volumetric soft light.`
